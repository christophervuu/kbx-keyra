## Change Requirements

- `ProjectActionsSection` receives: `projectId`, `mappingCount`, `schemaCount`, `onCreateMapping`, `onAddSchema`, `onDuplicateProject`, `onDeleteProject`
- Layout: Two groups separated visually:
  - **Primary actions**: "Create Mapping" (primary variant), "Add Schema" (secondary), "Duplicate Project" (secondary)
  - **Placeholders**: "Export Project" (disabled, ghost), "Import Mapping" (disabled, ghost) with "Coming in Phase 1" tooltip
  - **Danger zone**: "Delete Project" (danger variant), separated with a border or spacing
  - **Navigation**: "Project Settings" (ghost, with gear icon)
- Delete Project confirmation:
  - "This will delete {n} mappings and unlink {m} schemas. This action cannot be undone."
  - Uses `ConfirmDialog` from `features/mappings/components/` or a similar component
  - On confirm: calls `onDeleteProject()`
- Duplicate: calls `onDuplicateProject()` directly (no confirmation)
- Create Mapping / Add Schema: call respective handlers (navigate or trigger modal)
