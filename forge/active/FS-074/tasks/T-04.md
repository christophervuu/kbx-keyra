## Summary

Implement deterministic batch accept behavior for eligible multi-suggestion surfaces (for example Auto-Map workspace), including eligibility filtering, skip-reason reporting, and strict no-mutation guarantees for ineligible suggestions.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Batch accept eligibility rules (valid, non-stale, non-dismissed, feature-allowed).
- Deterministic apply sequencing and per-item result accounting.
- UI feedback summarizing applied vs skipped suggestions and reasons.
- Regression safeguards ensuring only eligible items mutate draft state.

### Out of Scope

- Per-item card/panel visual standardization.
- Backend AI generation logic changes.

---

- `ui/src/features/mappings/hooks/use-auto-map-workspace.ts`
- `ui/src/features/mappings/components/WorkspaceToolbar.tsx`

- `ui/src/features/mappings/**/*.test.tsx`

---

## Change Requirements

- Define deterministic eligibility filtering for batch accept.
- Enforce universal apply blocks in batch mode: invalid or stale suggestions are always skipped.
- Apply only eligible suggestions via explicit mutation path.
- Preserve ineligible suggestions untouched and report deterministic skip reasons.
- Ensure batch operations remain cancel-safe/error-resilient without silent side effects.

---

## Verification Requirements

- Tests for mixed eligibility sets and result accounting.
- Tests proving skipped/ineligible suggestions do not mutate mapping config.
- Manual validation of toolbar/batch UX messaging.

---

## Acceptance Checks

- Batch accept only applies eligible suggestions.
- Ineligible suggestions are skipped with clear reason reporting.
- No mapping mutation occurs for skipped items.
- Tests mapped to `AE-03` and `AE-05` pass.

---

## Blockers / Risks

- Eligibility policy decisions (stale/invalid apply rules) may remain open pending product alignment.
