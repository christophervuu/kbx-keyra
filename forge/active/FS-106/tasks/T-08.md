## Summary

Implement scheduled cleanup workflow that enforces per-environment retention counts, protects in-use artifacts, and updates rollback eligibility markers without relying on S3 lifecycle count logic.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Scheduled cleanup Lambda/Step Functions flow.
- Artifact protection checks (active, in-progress op, retention window, promotion-source requirement).
- Local-copy-only deletion semantics per environment.
- History rollback-eligibility updates.

### Out of Scope

- Temporary object lifecycle policy tuning.

---

- `src/lambda/deployment/*cleanup*` (new)

## Change Requirements

- Apply retention counts: DEV=20, PREPROD=20, PROD=50 successful activations.
- Do not delete protected artifacts.
- Allow deleting obsolete DEV local copy even if same artifact identity is active in PROD local runtime.

---

## Verification Requirements

- Unit tests for retention ordering/protection rules.
- Integration tests for cleanup behavior and rollback eligibility updates.

---

## Acceptance Checks

- Cleanup never deletes active or in-use local artifact.
- Cleanup may remove obsolete DEV local copy independent of PROD copy.
- History rows reflect rollback eligibility after cleanup.

---

## Blockers / Risks

- Incorrect protection logic can degrade rollback safety.

---

## Notes

- S3 lifecycle remains for temporary/transient object classes only.
