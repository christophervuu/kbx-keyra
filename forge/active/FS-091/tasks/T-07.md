## Summary

Execute benchmark and parity validation plan (small/medium/large schemas including 23k-field case) to confirm latency and quality thresholds before OpenSearch decommission.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Define and run representative retrieval benchmarks by schema size.
- Analyze shadow-mode quality deltas against baseline period.
- Produce cutover readiness evidence and rollback criteria.

### Out of Scope

- Infrastructure resource deletion.
- Documentation architecture updates.

## Change Requirements

- Latency measurements are reproducible and segmented by schema-size tier.
- Quality proxy analysis compares pre/post baseline consistently.
- Shadow parity gates are enforced as:
  - Top-K Jaccard overlap (K=10) average >= 0.70 (canonical gate)
  - NDCG@10 delta >= -0.10 (safety gate)
- Cutover decision criteria are explicit and auditable.

---

## Verification Requirements

- Automated benchmark/test runs where practical.
- Manual review report for acceptance-rate and overlap metrics.
- Lint/typecheck/test for any touched test harness code.

---

## Acceptance Checks

- Report demonstrates p95 latency targets met (or clearly flags blockers).
- Quality drop is within agreed threshold or escalation is documented.
- Report includes Jaccard@10 and NDCG@10-delta outcomes against thresholds.
- Go/no-go criteria and rollback triggers are recorded.

---

## Blockers / Risks

- Lack of representative production-like traffic/query corpus may bias results.

---

## Notes

- This task is the migration gate before OpenSearch decommission.
