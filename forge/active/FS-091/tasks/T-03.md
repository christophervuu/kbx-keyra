## Summary

Add second-stage optional rerank (cosine similarity over capped candidates) and bounded parent/sibling/children context enrichment for prompt assembly. This task completes retrieval quality behavior beyond lexical lookup.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Query embedding computation once per request.
- Candidate-only cosine rerank path with configurable weighting blend.
- Deterministic blended scoring with lexical/path/type boost integration.
- Bounded structural expansion (parent/sibling/children) with explicit caps.

### Out of Scope

- Feature-level acceptance-rate analytics implementation.
- OpenSearch fallback behavior.

## Change Requirements

- Rerank can be toggled/disabled without failing retrieval.
- When disabled or embeddings unavailable, lexical ranking remains authoritative.
- Embeddings remain per-node in DynamoDB in this phase; implement guardrail instrumentation for item-size/read-cost pressure to support hybrid S3 fallback decision.
- Context expansion limits are always applied and test-covered.

## Verification Requirements
