## Summary

Verify and complete mapping engine support for declared external aliases so enrichment inputs are validated and produce clear diagnostics at validation and runtime, without changing core engine purity or introducing I/O.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Validation pass behavior for `external("alias")` declaration checks.
- Runtime diagnostic behavior when declared required enrichment payloads are absent.
- Function-level tests for `external()` + `get()` against enrichment payload objects.

### Out of Scope

- New DSL syntax.
- Non-enrichment engine redesign.

---

## Relevant Areas

- `src/engine/validate/constants-externals.ts`
- `src/engine/functions/source-access.ts`
- `src/engine/execute.ts` ?
- tests under `tests/engine/validate/*` and `tests/engine/execute*`

---

## Change Requirements

- Validation must report undeclared alias references deterministically.
- Runtime behavior for missing enrichment context must be explicit and documented.
- Existing `source()`, `constant()`, and static behavior must remain unchanged.

---

## Verification Requirements

- Unit tests for validation diagnostics on declared/undeclared aliases.
- Execution tests for enrichment present/missing paths and mixed source+external expressions.
- Full engine test/lint/typecheck on touched modules.

---

## Acceptance Checks

- `external()` declaration validation is deterministic and test-covered.
- `get(external("alias"), "path")` works for enrichment payload fields.
- Missing enrichment diagnostics are clear and non-regressive for existing mappings.

---

## Blockers / Risks

- Required/optional semantics may span runtime-layer policy decisions outside pure engine context.
