## Summary

Rewrite generation rules so only explicit recipe operands participate, recipe order drives output order, and tray insertion order never implicitly defines recipe membership.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Generator logic for direct/fixed/constant/concat/coalesce/calculation/conditional/value-map/array handoff wrappers.
- Remove fallback-to-all-input behavior when recipe operands are absent.
- Preserve registered DSL-only emission.

### Out of Scope

- UI layout changes.

---

## Relevant Areas

- `ui/src/features/mappings/lib/smart-builder-expression-generator.ts`
- `ui/src/features/mappings/lib/smart-builder-expression-generator.test.ts`

---

## Change Requirements

- No implicit concat/math/coalesce from tray size.
- No generated replacement DSL when recipe incomplete.

---

## Verification Requirements

- Deterministic generator tests for operand ordering and omission behavior.

---

## Acceptance Checks

- Existing insertion-order fallback removed for recipe kinds that require explicit values.
- All covered recipe outputs are deterministic across repeated runs.

---

## Blockers / Risks

- Risk of regressions in older tests assuming implicit tray behavior.
