## Summary

Wire the evaluator's public surface (`evaluate`, `resolvePath`, evaluator types) into the barrel exports from `src/engine/dsl/index.ts` and ensure they are accessible from `src/engine/index.ts`. This makes the evaluator consumable by downstream modules (execute.ts, validate.ts) and external consumers.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Update `src/engine/dsl/index.ts` to export `evaluate` from `evaluator.ts`
- Update `src/engine/dsl/index.ts` to export `resolvePath` from `resolve-path.ts`
- Update `src/engine/dsl/index.ts` to export evaluator types (EvaluationContext, EvaluationResult, ScopeEntry, EvaluatorTraceEntry, etc.)
- Verify `src/engine/index.ts` re-exports make the evaluator accessible at the top level
- Verify TraceVerbosity is accessible from `src/engine/` (exported via types/options)
- Confirm no circular dependency introduced

### Out of Scope

- Implementing evaluate() or resolvePath() logic (done in T-02, T-03)
- Writing new tests (T-05)
- Modifying execute.ts or validate.ts to consume the evaluator (that's FS-007)
